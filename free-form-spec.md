### applove free Form Spec

Summary

Applove is blackchain-driven stake based crowdfunding platform for funding development of apps, extensions etc, anything that has an app store. Investors can fund app development by purchasing a profit share stake in app. Contracts and funds distribution is via smart contracts. App store is used as an Oracle. One way to think of it is blockchain based Startengine.com. A big part of the app is AI based app profit prediction engine.

The app consist of investor site and app developer site. 

Investor site has an app gallery and all the other supporting investment, reporting and AI interaction screens. 

Developer site allows app submission and all the other supporting investment, reporting and AI interaction screens.

Talking economics, blockchain architecture etc is TBD.
